AI Engineer / AI Developer – Intern & Fresher

Company: Wizcoder AI Labs Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Surat, Gujarat
Experience: Internship / Fresher
Employment Type: Full-Time / Internship
Work Mode: On-Site

About the Role

We are looking for an enthusiastic AI Engineer / AI Developer – Intern or Fresher to join our team in Surat.

This opportunity is suitable for candidates who have a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, LLMs, AI agents, and automation and want to work on practical AI-powered applications.

You will get an opportunity to work with modern AI models, APIs, tools, and workflows while contributing to real projects.

Key Responsibilities:

Assist in building and testing AI-powered features and applications.
Work with AI model APIs such as OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, and similar platforms.
Support the development of chatbots, AI agents, automation workflows, and Generative AI features.
Write clean and well-documented Python or JavaScript code.
Test, debug, and improve AI prompts and workflows for accuracy and reliability.
Assist in integrating AI features into existing products, websites, and applications.
Document experiments, findings, and technical processes.
Participate in daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and code reviews.
Stay updated with new AI tools, models, and techniques relevant to ongoing projects.

Required Qualifications:

Currently pursuing B.Tech / B.E. / BCA / MCA / M.Sc. in Computer Science, IT, AI, or a related field.
Final-year students are preferred.
Solid understanding of Python or JavaScript programming.
Basic understanding of how AI models and LLMs work, including inputs, prompts, outputs, and APIs.
Understanding of Data Structures, Algorithms, and OOP concepts.
Comfortable working with APIs and JSON data.

Preferred / Good-to-Have Skills:

Experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar tools for building projects.
Exposure to Prompt Engineering or building AI agents/workflows.
Familiarity with LangChain, LlamaIndex, n8n, or Make.
Knowledge of SQL and databases.
Familiarity with Git/GitHub.
Personal or academic AI projects, hackathon experience, or open-source contributions.
Basic understanding of AWS, GCP, or Azure.
Knowledge of REST APIs and frameworks such as Flask, FastAPI, or Node.js.

Who Can Apply?

AI/ML or AI/Developer freshers
Final-year students
Recent graduates
Candidates with relevant academic or personal AI projects
Candidates with internship experience in AI/ML or Generative AI

Location Requirement:

This is an on-site opportunity in Surat, Gujarat.

Candidates who are currently based in Surat or are willing to work on-site in Surat are encouraged to apply.

What We Value:

We are looking for candidates who are curious, willing to learn, interested in new AI technologies, and excited to work on practical AI projects.

If you have built any AI-related project, chatbot, AI agent, automation workflow, or GenAI project, please mention it in your resume/application.

Apply now and start your journey in AI with Wizcoder AI Labs Pvt. Ltd.

Pay: ₹7,000.00 - ₹8,000.00 per month

Work Location: In person
